# FORMULAK

## What it is
FORMULAK (فۆڕمولا کەی) is an independent Sorani Kurdish news and data site about Formula 1 first, then the wider formula racing world and its industry. An automated system finds news and writes it in Sorani; the owner, Shwan, approves every story before it is published.

## Register
product

## Audience
Sorani-speaking Formula 1 fans, mostly on phones, in the Kurdistan Region and the diaspora. Many follow F1 through English or Arabic sources today, or through social posts. They come to answer: what happened, who won, where does my driver stand, when is the next session in my time.

## Job
Be the place a Sorani reader checks first on a race weekend and every day between them: news, results, standings, calendar, driver and team pages, in their own language.

## Principles
- Facts first. Results, positions, points and times are copied from the source, never worked out.
- Every source is credited. Transfers are "reported" until confirmed.
- Right to left, Sorani first, Latin names stay Latin. Times in Hewlêr time.
- Phone first.

## Brand constraints
FORMULAK wordmark with custom K (src/components/logo-paths.ts). Red #E10600, black #0A0A0A, white, charcoal #171717, grey #8A8A8A. No gradients. No Kurdish symbols, sunburst, flags, car silhouettes, checkered flags or F1 lettering. Rabar for Sorani. Footer carries the independent-site disclaimer.

## Never
Race footage, broadcast screenshots, onboard clips, team radio, F1 audio. Invented results. Rumours as fact.
